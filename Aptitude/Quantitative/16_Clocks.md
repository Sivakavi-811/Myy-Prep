# 16 — Clocks

## Key Formulas & Shortcuts

| Concept | Value / Formula |
|---|---|
| Minute hand speed | **6° per minute** |
| Hour hand speed | **0.5° per minute** (30° per hour) |
| Relative speed of the two hands | **5.5° per minute** |
| Angle at H hours M minutes | **θ = \|30H − 5.5M\|** (if θ > 180°, use 360° − θ) |
| Hands coincide | Every **65 5/11 minutes**, which is **22 times** a day |
| Hands at a right angle | **44 times** a day |
| Hands opposite (180°) | **22 times** a day |

> **Shortcut:** The hands are together during hour H at **M = (60/11) × H** minutes past H.

---

## Question 1

Find the angle between the hands at **3:40**.

### Solution

1. θ = |30 × 3 − 5.5 × 40| = |90 − 220| = **130°**

### ✅ Answer: **130°**

---

## Question 2

At what time between **4 and 5 o'clock** are the hands **together**?

### Solution

1. At 4:00 the hour hand is 120° ahead of the minute hand
2. The minute hand gains 5.5° per minute, so it takes 120 / 5.5 = **240/11 = 21 9/11 minutes** to catch up
3. Shortcut check: (60/11) × 4 = 240/11 ✔

### ✅ Answer: **21 9/11 minutes past 4**

---

## Question 3

A clock gains **5 minutes every hour**. It is set right at **8:00 AM**. What time does it show at **2:00 PM** (true time) the same day?

### Solution

1. From 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM is **6 hours** of true time
2. It gains 6 × 5 = **30 minutes**
3. It shows 2:00 PM + 30 minutes = **2:30 PM**

### ✅ Answer: **2:30 PM**

---

## Takeaways

- Remember **30H − 5.5M**. It answers most angle questions.
- For "together", "opposite", or "right angle" questions, divide the gap that needs closing by **5.5° per minute**.
- In fast or slow clock questions, measure the elapsed **true** time first.
