# 09 — Problems on Ages

## Key Ideas & Shortcuts

| Concept | Rule |
|---|---|
| Age difference | Two people's age difference **never changes** over time |
| n years ago / hence | Subtract or add **n** to **each** person's age |
| Ratio problems | Write the ages as **ak** and **bk**, then solve for k |
| Sum of ages after n years | Increases by **n × (number of people)** |

> **Tip:** When the answer is multiple choice, testing the options is often faster than setting up equations.

---

## Question 1

A father is **30 years older** than his son. In **5 years**, the father will be **3 times** as old as the son. Find their present ages.

### Solution

1. Let the son's age be x, so the father's age is x + 30
2. In 5 years: x + 35 = 3(x + 5)
3. x + 35 = 3x + 15, so 2x = 20 and x = **10**
4. The son is 10 and the father is 40

**Check:** in 5 years they will be 15 and 45, and 45 = 3 × 15 ✔

### ✅ Answer: **Son 10 years, Father 40 years**

## Question 2

The present ages of A and B are in the ratio **4 : 5**. **6 years ago**, the ratio was **2 : 3**. Find their present ages.

### Solution

1. Present ages are 4k and 5k
2. (4k − 6) / (5k − 6) = 2/3
3. 3(4k − 6) = 2(5k − 6), so 12k − 18 = 10k − 12, so 2k = 6 and k = **3**
4. A = 12 and B = 15

**Check:** 6 years ago they were 6 and 9, and 6 : 9 = 2 : 3 ✔

### ✅ Answer: **A = 12 years, B = 15 years**

## Question 3

The sum of the ages of **5 children** born **3 years apart** is **50 years**. What is the age of the youngest child?

### Solution

1. The ages are x, x+3, x+6, x+9, x+12
2. Sum = 5x + 30 = 50, so x = **4**

### ✅ Answer: **4 years**

---

## Takeaways

- Use one variable and express every other age in terms of it.
- When moving through time, change **every** age by the same amount. The difference between ages stays the same.
- Always put the answer back into the original conditions to check it.
