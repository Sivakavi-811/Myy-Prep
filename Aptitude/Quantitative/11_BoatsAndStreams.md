# 11 — Boats and Streams

## Key Formulas & Shortcuts

Let **b** = speed of the boat in still water and **s** = speed of the stream.

| Concept | Formula |
|---|---|
| Downstream speed (with the current) | **D = b + s** |
| Upstream speed (against the current) | **U = b − s** |
| Boat speed in still water | **b = (D + U) / 2** |
| Stream speed | **s = (D − U) / 2** |
| Same distance d down and back up | Total time = **d/(b+s) + d/(b−s)** |

> **Tip:** Most questions give you D and U, either directly or through distance ÷ time. After that, the half-sum and half-difference formulas give the answer straight away.

---

## Question 1

A boat goes **24 km downstream in 3 hours** and **24 km upstream in 6 hours**. Find the speed of the boat and of the stream.

### Solution

1. D = 24/3 = **8 km/h** and U = 24/6 = **4 km/h**
2. b = (8 + 4)/2 = **6 km/h**
3. s = (8 − 4)/2 = **2 km/h**

### ✅ Answer: **Boat 6 km/h, Stream 2 km/h**

---

## Question 2

A man rows at **10 km/h** in still water. The river flows at **2 km/h**. He takes **5 hours** in total to row to a place and back. How far away is the place?

### Solution

1. D = 12 km/h and U = 8 km/h
2. d/12 + d/8 = 5
3. LCM is 24: (2d + 3d)/24 = 5, so 5d = 120 and d = **24 km**

### ✅ Answer: **24 km**

---

## Question 3

A swimmer takes **twice as long** to swim upstream as to swim the same distance downstream. Find the ratio of the swimmer's speed in still water to the stream speed.

### Solution

1. Over the same distance, time is inversely proportional to speed. So **D = 2U**
2. b + s = 2(b − s), so b = 3s
3. b : s = **3 : 1**

### ✅ Answer: **3 : 1**

---

## Takeaways

- Change every situation into D and U first. The rest is the half-sum and half-difference formulas.
- For the same distance, the ratio of times is the **inverse** of the ratio of speeds.
- Upstream speed must stay positive (b > s), or the boat can't move forward.
