# 02 — Blood Relations

## Approach & Shortcuts

| Step | What to do |
|---|---|
| 1 | Draw a **family tree**: generations go top to bottom, spouses side by side |
| 2 | Use symbols: **+ male**, **− female**, **=** for married couples, a vertical line for parent → child |
| 3 | Always decode from the **end of the sentence** ("my father's only son" → start from "my father") |
| 4 | Don't assume anyone's gender unless the question states it |

### Common relations to know

| Phrase | Means |
|---|---|
| Father's or mother's brother | Uncle |
| Father's or mother's sister | Aunt |
| Brother's or sister's son / daughter | Nephew / Niece |
| Spouse's brother / sister | Brother-in-law / Sister-in-law |
| Father's only son (said by a male) | **Himself** |

---

## Question 1

Pointing to a man, Riya says, **"He is the son of my grandfather's only son."** How is the man related to Riya?

### Solution

1. "My grandfather's only son" is Riya's **father**.
2. "The son of my father" is Riya's **brother**.

### ✅ Answer: **Brother**

---

## Question 2

A is the brother of B. B is the sister of C. C is the father of D. How is A related to D?

### Solution

1. A, B and C are siblings. A is male, B is female, and C is male because he is a father.
2. C is D's father, so A is a brother of D's father.

```
   A(+)  B(−)  C(+)
                |
                D
```

### ✅ Answer: **A is D's uncle** (paternal uncle)

---

## Question 3

Introducing a woman, Arun says, **"Her mother is the only daughter of my mother."** How is Arun related to the woman?

### Solution

1. "The only daughter of my mother" is Arun's **sister**.
2. The woman's mother is Arun's sister.
3. So Arun is the brother of the woman's mother.

### ✅ Answer: **Maternal uncle**

---

## Takeaways

- Decode the sentence **backward**, from the speaker's side.
- Mark genders as soon as the question tells you. Many answers depend on gender.
- For questions with more than three people, draw the tree. Keeping it in your head leads to mistakes.
